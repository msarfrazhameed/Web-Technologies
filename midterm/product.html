Family Ribeye Steak Feast

Rs. 4500

Includes farm-fresh ribeye cuts, rosemary garlic butter, baby potatoes, and crisp green beans for 4 people.

Full Meal Kit Description & Nutritional Breakdown

Bring the steakhouse experience directly to your kitchen table in just 25 minutes. Our Family Ribeye Steak Feast is crafted with pasture-raised beef, paired with aromatic fresh herbs and crisp seasonal vegetables harvested at peak ripeness.

Every HelloFresh box arrives insulated with ice packs to keep your ingredients fresh at your doorstep. We pre-portion every spice, sauce, and produce item so you experience zero food waste and effortless preparation.

What's Inside the Box

  • 4x Grass-Fed Ribeye Steaks (250g each)
  • 500g Baby Golden Potatoes
  • 300g Fresh French Green Beans
  • 2 Cloves Organic Garlic & Fresh Rosemary Sprigs
  • Chef's Signature Pan-Sear Herb Butter Pack

Step-by-Step Chef Instructions

Step 1: Wash and dry all fresh produce. Cut baby potatoes in half and toss with olive oil, salt, and cracked black pepper. Roast at 220°C for 20 minutes until golden and crispy.

Step 2: Pat the ribeye steaks dry with paper towels and season generously. Sear in a hot cast-iron skillet for 3 to 4 minutes per side, basting with melted garlic rosemary butter during the final minute.

Step 3: Blanch the green beans until tender-crisp, plate alongside the rested steak and roasted potatoes, and drizzle with the remaining pan sauce. Enjoy restaurant-quality dining at home!

Nutritional Information per serving: 680 kcal, 48g Protein, 34g Carbohydrates, 38g Healthy Fats. Certified Halal & sustainably sourced.
